# Code of Conduct

Privio's community spaces — this repository, its issue tracker, pull requests
and any official channel — exist so people can build a private messenger
together. They are meant to be places where contributing does not cost you
your peace.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful, and assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about
  trade-offs, not motives.
* Critique ideas and code, not people. "This leaks the recipient's device id"
  is a review. "Did you even read the spec" is not.
* Accept that maintainers decide what fits the project's scope. A well-argued
  proposal can still be declined.
* Take the extra minute for someone who is new. Every person who understands
  this codebase once did not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
* Publishing someone's private information — real name, address, employer,
  accounts — without their explicit consent. In a privacy project this is not
  an ironic offence, it is a disqualifying one.
* Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly reopening a decision that
  has been made and explained.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and to any conduct outside them that is
directed at a participant because of their involvement here.

## Enforcement

Reports go to **conduct@privio.com**. Include what happened, where, and links
if there are any. If your report concerns a maintainer, say so — it will be
handled by someone else.

Reports are kept confidential. We will not share a reporter's identity with
the person reported without asking first.

Maintainers may warn, edit or remove content, and temporarily or permanently
ban an account from project spaces. Actions are explained to the person
affected. Enforcement is not conditional on how valuable someone's
contributions are.
